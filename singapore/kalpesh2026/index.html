Little India & Kampong Glam

Saturday · 21 March · V Hotel Lavender

Morning — Little India
9 AM
MTR — South Indian Breakfast
Start the day at MTR (Mavalli Tiffin Rooms) on Serangoon Road — a legendary South Indian institution famous for its masala dosa, idli and filter coffee. Arrive early; tables fill up fast on weekends.
Food
10 AM
Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple
Free entry. One of Singapore's most vibrant Hindu temples, dedicated to Goddess Kali. The towering gopuram is richly carved and quite spectacular. Remove footwear before entering.
Sightseeing
10:30 AM
House of Tan Teng Niah
A short walk away — the last surviving Chinese villa in Little India, painted in dazzling candy-bright colours. One of Singapore's most photographed heritage buildings. Great family photo stop.
Sightseeing
11 AM
Indian Heritage Centre
Campbell Lane. A beautifully designed museum tracing the journey of Indian communities to Southeast Asia. Interactive exhibits, textiles, trade history and cultural artefacts. Allow 45–60 mins. Tickets ~S$8 adults.
Sightseeing
12:30 PM
Lunch — Shivam Restaurant
Lovely vegetarian restaurant next to Mustafa Centre, serving Gujarati thali, North Indian, Chinese and Mexican cuisine.
Food
2 PM
Mustafa Centre — Shopping
The legendary 24-hour department store of Little India. Electronics, perfumes, watches, spices, gold jewellery, saris and everything else — all under one roof at competitive prices. A uniquely Singapore experience. Allow 1–1.5 hrs.
Shopping
3:30 PM
Little India Arcade — Souvenirs
48 Serangoon Road. A heritage shophouse arcade bursting with traditional Indian goods — silk textiles, brass idols, flower garlands, incense and hand-crafted jewellery. Perfect for picking up authentic souvenirs.
Shopping
Evening — Kampong Glam
5:30 PM
Sultan Mosque
Singapore's most iconic mosque, with its striking golden onion dome. Non-Muslims may enter the main hall outside prayer times — modest dress required (robes available at the entrance). The exterior is beautiful at dusk.
Sightseeing
6 PM
Haji Lane — Evening Stroll
The narrow pastel-painted street comes alive after dark with indie boutiques, street art and café culture. Perfect for the teens to explore and photograph. Shops open till 10pm. Arab Street alongside has excellent textile and carpet shops.
SightseeingShopping
7:30 PM
Dinner at Kampong Glam
Zamzam Restaurant (authentic Malay-Muslim, est. 1908, on North Bridge Road) is a local institution. Alternatively, the Arab Street strip has Turkish, Lebanese and Middle Eastern options to suit all tastes.
Food

Marina Bay — Full Immersion

Sunday · 22 March · Mastercard Day MC Offers

Morning
9 AM
Merlion Park
Take MRT to Bayfront. Start at the iconic Merlion statue on the waterfront — free, and the backdrop of MBS across the water is Singapore's most photographed view. Walk the promenade; the morning light is superb here before crowds arrive.
Sightseeing
10 AM
Sands Lifestyle Membership Registration MC Essential
Head to Marina Bay Sands, toward The Shoppes B1 Sampan Ride area and find the Sands Lifestyle Counter. Register your Mastercard for dining, spa and retail benefits. Takes 10 mins. Open 10am–11pm. Book your attraction tickets from the Sands Lifestyle Membership app.
Mastercard
10:30 AM
ArtScience Museum MC Offer
6 Bayfront Ave. Present your Mastercard at the ticketing counter for the offer. The permanent teamLab Future World exhibition is the centrepiece — fully immersive digital art rooms that genuinely stun all ages. The lotus-shaped building itself is iconic. Allow 2–2.5 hrs.
CultureMastercard
1:30 PM
Sampan Ride & Digital Light Canvas Optional
While in The Shoppes Level B1, you can take the Sampan boat ride through the canal — a fun 10-min gondola experience. The adjacent teamLab Digital Light Canvas is a free, stunning large-scale interactive art installation you simply walk through.
ActivityOptional
Afternoon — Gardens by the Bay
2:30 PM
Cloud Forest
18 Marina Gardens Drive. A 35m indoor mountain wrapped in lush greenery with a dramatic waterfall at its core. Elevator to the top, then walkways spiral down through mist and rare highland plants. Currently features a Jurassic World themed experience. Allow 1–1.5 hrs.
Nature
4 PM
Flower Dome
The world's largest glass greenhouse — cool Mediterranean and semi-arid plant biomes from across the globe. Beautifully curated seasonal floral displays. Buy the Cloud Forest + Flower Dome combo ticket for best value.
Nature
Evening — Sunset & Dinner
5:30 PM
Observation Deck — Sunset MC Offer
Tower 3, Level 57, Marina Bay Sands. Take the dedicated lift from the Tower 3 hotel lobby. Present Mastercard at the ticketing desk. Arrive at 5:30–6pm to watch the city turn gold at sunset — the panorama of Gardens by the Bay, the Straits of Malacca and the full skyline is unmatched. Open until 10pm.
ViewsMastercard
7:30 PM
Dinner — Ce La Vi
Level 57, Tower 3, Marina Bay Sands — immediately after the Observation Deck. Ce La Vi is the signature rooftop restaurant and skybar at MBS. Contemporary Asian cuisine with sweeping 360° views. Dress smart-casual. Reservations strongly recommended. Mains S$45–80 per person.
DiningSignature
8:45 PM
Garden Rhapsody — Supertree Light Show If energy allows
Free. Shows nightly at 7:45pm and 8:45pm at the Supertree Grove. A short walk from MBS. The 8:45pm show works perfectly after Ce La Vi dinner. Lie on the grass beneath the illuminated Supertrees as music and light play overhead.
FreeNight Show

Zoo · National Museum · Orchard · Clarke Quay

Monday · 23 March · City-Wide Exploration

Morning
8:30 AM
Singapore Zoo
80 Mandai Lake Road. One of the world's best open-concept zoos — no cages, just natural landscapes and moated enclosures. Must-sees: the giant pandas KaiKai & JiaJia, Fragile Forest biodome and the Orangutan habitat. Take a Grab taxi (~30 mins from hotel). Allow 3 hrs. Open from 8:30am.
NatureFamily
Afternoon
12:30 PM
Lunch — Mandai or Orchard
Quick lunch at the zoo's food court before heading back to town, or grab a meal near Orchard Road on arrival. Take MRT from Mandai — bus to Khatib MRT (NS line) then south to Orchard (~40 mins total).
Food
2 PM
National Museum of Singapore
93 Stamford Road. Singapore's oldest museum in a spectacular neoclassical building. The Singapore History Gallery traces the island's story from 14th-century trading post to modern metropolis. ~S$15 for tourists. Allow 1–1.5 hrs.
CultureHistory
4 PM
Orchard Road — Shopping
Singapore's legendary 2.2km retail boulevard. ION Orchard, Takashimaya, Paragon, 313@somerset and Orchard Central are the headline malls. Great for fashion, luxury brands, tech and F&B. Allow 1.5–2 hrs.
Shopping
Evening
7 PM
Dinner & Evening — Clarke Quay
Take MRT or Grab to Clarke Quay (CC/NE line). Singapore's most vibrant riverside dining precinct — colourful restored shophouses along the Singapore River with dozens of restaurants and live music. Try Jumbo Seafood for the legendary chilli crab — book ahead.
DiningNightlife
9 PM
River Walk — Boat Quay
After dinner, stroll the riverside walkway from Clarke Quay to Boat Quay — a 10-min walk along the Singapore River lit beautifully at night with the skyline as a backdrop.
Free

Fort Canning & Board Genting Dream

Tuesday · 24 March · Departure Day

9 AM
Fort Canning Park
Free. A hilltop heritage park in the heart of the city — shaded walks, old colonial gates, spice gardens, cannons and great views. A serene final morning on the mainland. MRT to Dhoby Ghaut, then 10-min walk uphill.
11 AM
Hotel Checkout
Check out of V Lavender. Store luggage at hotel or carry directly — you'll transfer to the cruise terminal this afternoon. Confirm embarkation time with Genting Cruises.
12 PM
Final Lunch — Maxwell Food Centre
Singapore's most famous hawker centre in Chinatown. One last truly local meal — Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice is a must. Budget S$8–12 per person for a full meal.
2:30 PM
Board Genting Dream Cruise
Marina Bay Cruise Centre (MBCC), 61 Marina Coastal Drive. Grab taxi ~15 mins. Bring passport + booking confirmation. Note: MBCC is different from Tanah Merah ferry terminal — specify Marina Bay Cruise Centre to your driver.
Embarkation Tips
Specify Marina Bay Cruise Centre — not Tanah Merah — to Grab driver
Porters at terminal — tip S$2–3 per bag is appreciated
Buy Singapore dollars before boarding — onboard rates less favourable
Don't forget items from the hotel safe before checkout

Oceanarium & Adventure Cove Waterpark

Saturday · 28 March · Resorts World Sentosa Day

9 AM
Singapore Oceanarium
Opens 9am. One of the world's largest aquariums. The Open Ocean Habitat panel is enormous — sit and watch sharks, rays and thousands of fish drift past. Arrive early to beat Saturday crowds. Allow 2–2.5 hrs.
12 PM
Lunch at RWS
Multiple options within Resorts World between the two attractions. Malaysian Hawker food court is a great bet. Allow 45 mins to refuel and reapply sunscreen before the waterpark.
1 PM
Adventure Cove Waterpark
Closes 5pm. Highlights: Riptide Rocket (Asia's first hydro-magnetic coaster), Adventure River lazy float, wave pool and Rainbow Reef snorkelling among tropical fish. Grab lockers immediately on arrival. Reapply SPF50+ every 60 mins.
5:30 PM
Sunset at Central Beach Bazaar
Food kiosks right on Siloso Beach — grilled corn by the sea. Perfect low-key evening. Optional: Skyline Luge for some extra adrenaline.
Waterpark Tips
Book Adventure Cove online — S$5 cheaper, no morning queue
Rent locker near the wave pool — most central point
Rainbow Reef: check mask seal before entering water
Bring water shoes — pool deck gets very hot by afternoon

Universal Studios & Wings of Time

Sunday · 29 March · Full Adventure Day

9:45 AM
Universal Studios Singapore
Open 10am–8pm. Arrive at gates before opening. Must-dos: Revenge of the Mummy (indoor coaster), Transformers: The Ride (4D), Battlestar Galactica (dual coasters for teens), Jurassic World Raptor Encounter, WaterWorld Live Stunt Show (sit in the soak zone!).
6 PM
Dinner at RWS or VivoCity
Head to VivoCity (free Sentosa Express + 5 mins) for a wider range of restaurants, or eat within Resorts World. Budget S$20–35 per person for a sit-down meal.
7:10 PM
Wings of Time Fireworks Symphony
Siloso Beach. Shows at 7:40pm and 8:40pm nightly. Arrive 25 mins before for good seats — first come, first served. A 20-min spectacular: water screens, lasers, fire and fireworks set to music. Singapore's only nightly fireworks show.
USS Tips
Pre-book via Klook — saves ~S$10–15 per person and avoids queues
Hit Mummy + Transformers first before queues build after 11am
Express Passes worth it on Sundays — can get very busy
Wings of Time: 7:40pm show is ideal — cooler air, darker sky

Palawan Beach & Departure

Monday · 30 March · Final Day

MORN
Resort Breakfast & Pool
Leisurely last morning at the Shangri-La's stunning pool terrace. Savour breakfast without any rush — checkout typically at noon.
10 AM
The Palawan @ Sentosa
Beach tram to Palawan Beach. Walk the suspension bridge to the Southernmost Point of Continental Asia — a great photo op. Teens should try HydroDash, the floating water obstacle course just offshore.
12 PM
VivoCity → Changi Airport
Sentosa Express to VivoCity for last-minute shopping and a final lunch on the rooftop. Then Grab or MRT (HarbourFront → Tanah Merah → Changi). Allow 3 hrs before departure. Don't miss Jewel Changi's indoor waterfall if time permits!
Departure Tips
Changi Airport is 35–40 mins from Sentosa
Jewel Changi (Terminal 1) — 30 mins for the indoor waterfall is worth it
EZ-Link card: refund unused balance at any MRT ticket office
